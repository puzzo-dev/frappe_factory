Where ERPNext runs

The key's user needs the Manufacturing User, Stock User and Quality Manager roles on ERPNext.

What links

Work orders drive production runsA run starts from an ERPNext Work Order. Counts and times update its Job Card.
Post stock when a run endsA Manufacture Stock Entry with the good quantity; rejects go in as scrap.
Send stops as Downtime EntryEach closed machine stop becomes a Downtime Entry on its Workstation, with the operator and reason.
Machine state to Workstation statusSets Production, Idle, Problem or Maintenance, so ERPNext's Plant Floor matches the machines.
Lab samples as Quality InspectionTrip samples and shift checks create Quality Inspections against the batch.
Material requests from ResourcesRequest on a consumable creates a Material Request for the plant's store.
Employees and shifts from HRMSCrew and attendance on the shift handover. Needs HRMS on the ERPNext site.
